#### **Overview**

- **Goal:** Persuade through writing (using video, audio, and infographics as additional mediums).
    
- **Website Content Behavior:**
    
    - People **scan** web pages, rather than read in-depth (compared to print material).
        
    - Writing needs to be **concise and clear** for easy skimming.
        

---

#### **Key Tips for Writing for the Web:**

1. **Keep It Simple and Clear:**
    
    - Use simple, easy-to-understand words.
        
    - **Avoid jargon** or technical terms.
        
    - Write **short sentences** with **one main idea** per sentence.
        
2. **Use Headings and Sub-Headings:**
    
    - Highlight key information with **bold text**.
        
    - **Use bullets** for lists, instead of long paragraphs.
        

---

#### **Examples of Writing**

**Bad Example:**  
"In 2022, our company faced many challenges such as market decline and staff shortage. Despite all this, we achieved growth."

**Good Example:**  
"Our company grew in 2022, despite facing many challenges."

---

3. **Use Keywords (SEO):**
    
    - **Search Engine Optimization (SEO)**: Incorporate words people search on Google.
        
    - **Keyword usage:** Avoid overuse (keyword stuffing).
        
    - **Internal Links:** Add links to other relevant content within your website to improve SEO.
        
4. **Tone of Voice:**
    
    - **Friendly, direct language** that feels welcoming.
        
    - Professional but **approachable** tone.
        
    - **Use active voice** over passive voice for stronger statements.
        
    
    **Example of Voice:**
    
    - **Active voice:** "I wrote a letter."
        
    - **Passive voice:** "A letter was written by me."
        

---

#### **More Tips**

5. **Use Images:**
    
    - Support text with **relevant images**.
        
    - Avoid overloading the page with **too many pictures**.
        
    - Ensure images align with the content for better engagement.
        
    
    **Example:** A paragraph discussing village life with a corresponding image of village life.
    
6. **Update Your Web Content Regularly:**
    
    - Keep content **up-to-date**.
        
    - Remove **outdated** information.
        
    
    **Example:** If a sale has ended, or a product is out of stock, update or remove outdated details promptly.
