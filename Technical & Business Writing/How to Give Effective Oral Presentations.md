### **Stage #1: Planning**

**a) Audience:**

- Tailor your presentation to make it understandable and engaging for your specific audience.

**b) Introduce Yourself:**

- Begin by introducing **yourself** and your **topic**.
- Mention any **group members** involved.
- Provide an **outline** of your presentation (overview of topic and structure).
- Start with a **hook** (e.g., a surprising fact, a quote, or a storytelling technique) to capture attention.

**c) Structure Your Presentation:**

- **Introduction:** Provide background information on the topic.
- **Body:** Elaborate and discuss the main points (typically in 3 sections).
- **Conclusion:** Summarize and wrap up your presentation.

---

- **Important:** Never introduce new information in the conclusion.
- Use **transitional phrases** (e.g., "To sum up" or "In conclusion").
- End with a polite **thank you** and an invitation for questions: "Thank you for your time and patience. I welcome your questions."

---

### **Stage #2: Practicing**

- **Record yourself** to analyze your speech and pacing.
- **Practice in front of a mirror** or with peers to gauge your body language and presentation style.
- **Ask for feedback** from group members to improve your performance.

---

### **Stage #3: Presenting**

- Focus on **verbal communication**, **body language**, and **audience engagement**.

**Body Language Tips:**

- Pay attention to **facial expressions**, **eye contact**, **posture**, and **movement**.
- Maintain an appropriate **distance** from the audience for effective communication.

---

### **Additional Presentation Tips:**

1. **Follow the Dress Code**: Dress appropriately for the occasion.
2. **Voice Projection**: Speak clearly and loud enough for everyone to hear.
3. **Use Visuals Effectively**: Enhance your presentation with **slides**, **graphics**, **charts**, or **pictures**.
