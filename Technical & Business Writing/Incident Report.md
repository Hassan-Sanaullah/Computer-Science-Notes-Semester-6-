## 🔹 **What is an Incident Report?**

An **incident report** is a formal, written document used to record the details of unexpected events, accidents, or near misses. These reports are often filed in workplaces, schools, hospitals, or public spaces to document occurrences such as:

- **Injuries**
    
- **Property damage**
    
- **Security breaches**
    
- **Safety hazards**
    

The report serves as an official record and can be used for tracking incidents, assessing safety protocols, and supporting claims or investigations.

---

## 🔹 **Difference Between an Incident Report and a Police Report**

|**Feature**|**Incident Report**|**Police Report**|
|---|---|---|
|**Purpose**|To document internal events for safety, tracking, or insurance purposes.|To report a potential crime or legal violation to authorities.|
|**Prepared by**|Employees, supervisors, witnesses, or staff members.|Law enforcement officials or police officers.|
|**Used by**|Organizations, HR, management, safety officers.|Courts, police, prosecutors, and legal departments.|
|**Legal Standing**|For internal use, can support claims or investigations.|Formal legal document, often used to initiate legal proceedings.|
|**Example**|Workplace injury, property damage report.|Theft, assault, or other crimes reported to police.|

---

## 🔹 **Importance of an Incident Report**

1. **✅ Documentation** – Provides a factual record of the event, including details like time, location, and actions taken.
    
2. **🔒 Legal Protection** – Acts as a safeguard, particularly if the incident leads to an investigation or legal claim.
    
3. **🏥 Safety Improvement** – Helps identify hazards or areas of risk, promoting safer practices and policies.
    
4. **📝 Accountability** – Ensures responsible parties are identified, and follow-up actions are completed.
    
5. **📈 Data Analysis** – Enables organizations to track incidents over time, identify trends, and improve safety protocols.

## Example

### **INCIDENT REPORT**

**1. Report Number**: 2025-007

**2. Date of Incident**: June 15, 2025

**3. Time of Incident**: 10:30 AM

**4. Location of Incident**: Office - 2nd Floor Conference Room, XYZ Corporation

**5. Person(s) Involved**:

- **John Smith** (Employee, Marketing Department)
    
- **Sarah Davis** (Employee, Sales Department)
    

**6. Witnesses**:

- **Michael Green** (Employee, IT Department)
    
- **Amanda Roberts** (Employee, HR Department)
    

---

**7. Description of Incident**:  
At approximately 10:30 AM on June 15, 2025, during a meeting held in the 2nd Floor Conference Room, an accident occurred when **John Smith** tripped over a loose cable that was running across the floor near the entrance. The cable was connected to the projector, which had been set up for the meeting. As a result of the fall, **John Smith** lost balance and fell forward, hitting his elbow on the edge of the conference table. The incident caused him to experience immediate pain and discomfort in his left arm.

Both **Sarah Davis** and **Michael Green**, who were seated across from **John Smith**, witnessed the event. **Amanda Roberts**, who was also in the meeting, immediately alerted the HR department and called for assistance.

---

**8. Action Taken**:

- **Sarah Davis** and **Amanda Roberts** assisted **John Smith** by helping him to a chair and offering support while he assessed his condition.
    
- **Michael Green** quickly unplugged the projector to eliminate the risk of further injury from the exposed cable.
    
- **Amanda Roberts** contacted the HR department and the safety officer to report the injury and ensure proper first aid procedures.
    
- A first aid kit was brought to the room, and **John Smith** applied ice to his elbow. After about 20 minutes, he reported that the pain had subsided, but he was advised to seek medical attention for further evaluation.
    
- The safety officer arrived shortly after and completed an injury report.
    

---

**9. Reported By**:

- **Name**: Michael Green
    
- **Designation**: Employee, IT Department
    
- **Signature**: [Signed digitally]
    
- **Date**: June 15, 2025
