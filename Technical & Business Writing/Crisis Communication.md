## Crisis Communication:

- **What is it?**
    
    - A strategy for communicating during a disruptive event or crisis.
        
- **Why is it important?**
    
    - Quick, proactive, and detailed communication is critical during a crisis.
        
- **Key Points:**
    
    - Businesses need to act fast and communicate with both employees and external stakeholders.
        
    - Crisis teams focus on:
        
        - Protecting employees.
            
        - Reassuring the public.
            
        - Minimizing negative impacts.
---
## Crisis Communications Plan:

- **What is it?**
    
    - A strategic framework for handling communication during a crisis.
        
- **Purpose:**
    
    - To address communication challenges effectively and efficiently.
        
- **What it includes:**
    
    - Steps and protocols for the crisis communication team.
        
    - Ensures timely and accurate communication with stakeholders, media, and the public.
        
- **Goal:**
    
    - Mitigate damage to the organization’s reputation.
        
    - Maintain trust and transparency with employees and the public.
---
## Crisis Communication Strategies – Simple Notes:

- **Key Strategies:**
    
    1. **Move quickly** – Respond fast to the crisis.
        
    2. **Use appropriate communication channels** – Choose the best platforms for reaching your audience.
        
    3. **Create a crisis communication plan** – Have a clear plan in place for handling the crisis.
        
    4. **Offer crisis communication & management training** – Train teams in advance to handle crisis situations effectively.

## The stages of crisis communication
### Stage #1: Pre-crisis Phase – Simple Notes:

- **What it is:**
    
    - Planning and preparing for a crisis before it happens.
        
- **Two Key Areas:**
    
    1. **Prevention:** Reducing risks by identifying potential issues early.
        
    2. **Preparation:** Getting ready for crises, even if they’re unpredictable.
        
- **Prevention – How it Works:**
    
    - Identify emerging issues that could turn into crises.
        
    - Analyze and plan responses.
        
    - Communicate risks with stakeholders to prepare them.
        
- **Preparation – How to Do It:**
    
    - Can’t plan for every specific crisis, but general preparation is key.
        
    - **Steps for preparation:**
        
        - Form a crisis response team.
            
        - Designate a spokesperson.
            
        - Ensure effective communication among employees (e.g., using internal tools).
            
        - Create clear procedures and guidelines.
            
        - Provide training (e.g., crisis management, communication skills).

### Stage #2: Crisis Response Phase – Simple Notes:

- **Key Principles of Crisis Communication:**
    
    1. **Accurate** – Provide correct information.
        
    2. **Precise** – Be specific and to the point.
        
    3. **Clear** – Ensure the message is easy to understand.
        
    4. **Actionable** – Give information that leads to practical action.
        
- **Adjusting Information:**
    
    - As the crisis calms down, shift focus to:
        
        - Addressing the crisis’s **implications**.
            
        - Helping people cope **psychologically** with the aftermath.

### Stage #3: Post-crisis Communication – Simple Notes:

- **Why It’s Important:**
    
    - Communication shouldn’t stop once the crisis is "over" because its effects can last.
        
- **What It Involves:**
    
    - Extending previous communication efforts.
        
    - Sharing **lessons learned** from the crisis.
        
- **Main Goal:**
    
    - Manage reactions of different stakeholders (employees, public, etc.).
        
- **What Employees Want to Know Post-crisis:**
    
    1. **When** things will return to normal.
        
    2. **What** normalcy will look like.
        
    3. **How** the crisis impacted the organization.
        
    4. **What steps** will be taken to prevent future crises.

## Crisis Communication Example: Johnson & Johnson’s Response to the TYLENOL Crisis

- **What Happened:**
    
    - In 1982, 7 people died after consuming Extra Strength TYLENOL capsules laced with cyanide.
        
    - Although the issue was initially limited to Chicago, it posed a nationwide crisis.
        
- **How They Responded:**
    
    - **Immediate Action:**
        
        - Pulled **31 million bottles** off the shelves.
            
        - Launched a nationwide campaign to inform the public about the tampering.
            
    - Worked closely with **law enforcement** during the investigation.
        
- **Outcome:**
    
    - Introduced **tamper-evident packaging**—a first in the industry.
        
    - By being **transparent** and acting **swiftly**, Johnson & Johnson regained consumer trust and maintained market share.
