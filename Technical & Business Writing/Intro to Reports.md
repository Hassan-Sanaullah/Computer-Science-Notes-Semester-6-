### Introduction to Reports:

#### 1. **What is a Report?**

- A **report** is a written or spoken account of a specific topic.
    
- It's often an **official document** that outlines the facts, usually written by an expert or investigator.
    

#### 2. **What Kind of Information is Shared in Reports?**

- **Details** of events or situations.
    
- **Consequences** or ongoing effects.
    
- **Statistical data** or analytics.
    
- **Interpretations** of the data.
    
- **Predictions** or **recommendations**.
    
- How the information relates to other reports or events.
    
- **Note:** Reports are more focused and **fact-based**. Unlike essays, they are **scanned for specific insights** rather than read from start to finish.
    

---

### Types of Reports:

#### **Based on Direction of Communication:**

1. **Vertical Reports**:
    
    - Move **upward** (to higher management) or **downward** (to lower staff).
        
    - **Example:** Software team lead sends a project status report to the manager.
        
2. **Lateral Reports**:
    
    - Move **horizontally** between departments or teams.
        
    - **Example:** A network team shares an update with the cybersecurity team.
        

#### **Based on Formality:**

1. **Formal Reports**:
    
    - Structured, detailed, and official.
        
    - **Examples:** Project reports, research reports, feasibility studies.
        
2. **Informal Reports**:
    
    - Shorter, less structured (often emails or memos).
        
    - **Example:** A quick bug fix update to the team.
        

#### **Based on Purpose:**

1. **Informational Reports**:
    
    - Present **facts** without analysis or recommendations.
        
    - **Example:** Monthly system uptime report.
        
2. **Analytical Reports**:
    
    - Analyze situations and may suggest **actions**.
        
    - **Example:** A comparison of cloud service providers with a recommendation.
        

#### **Based on Function:**

1. **Proposal Reports**:
    
    - Suggest an **action plan** or idea for approval.
        
    - **Example:** Proposal to upgrade university servers.
        
2. **Progress Reports**:
    
    - Updates on ongoing projects or tasks.
        
    - **Example:** Report on the stages of a final year project.
        
3. **Feasibility Reports**:
    
    - Evaluate if a plan or idea is **practical**.
        
    - **Example:** Feasibility of switching programming frameworks.
        
4. **Incident Reports**:
    
    - Document unexpected **events** or issues.
        
    - **Example:** Reporting a network security breach.
        

---

### Components of a Technical/Business Report:

1. **Title Page**
    
2. **Executive Summary**
    
3. **Table of Contents**
    
4. **Introduction**
    
5. **Methodology**
    
6. **Main Body** (Findings/Discussion)
    
7. **Conclusion**
    
8. **Recommendations**
    
9. **References**
    
10. **Appendices**

## Example

### Example Report: Progress Update on Mobile App Development Project

#### **1. Title Page**

**Report Title:** Progress Update on Mobile App Development Project  
**Prepared By:** Software Development Team  
**Date:** June 18, 2025

---

#### **2. Executive Summary**

This progress report provides an update on the status of the mobile app development project, detailing the work completed, challenges faced, and the next steps. The project is on track to meet its launch deadline, with the user interface (UI) design and backend architecture now fully developed. However, some issues have arisen regarding integration with third-party payment services, which are currently being addressed. The app’s beta testing phase is scheduled to begin in two weeks, and the final version is expected to launch within the next two months.

---

#### **3. Table of Contents**

1. Introduction
    
2. Methodology
    
3. Work Completed
    
4. Current Issues
    
5. Next Steps
    
6. Conclusion
    
7. Recommendations
    
8. References
    
9. Appendices
    

---

#### **4. Introduction**

The purpose of this report is to provide an update on the development progress of the mobile app for our e-commerce platform. The project started on March 1, 2025, with an anticipated timeline of six months for completion. This report focuses on the progress made so far, the challenges encountered, and outlines the upcoming tasks necessary to meet the scheduled launch date.

---

#### **5. Methodology**

The progress update is based on weekly sprint reviews and status meetings held by the development team. Additionally, key performance metrics such as the completion of user stories, bugs reported, and the percentage of milestones achieved were tracked using project management software (Jira). The data for this report is sourced from these sprint reviews and testing logs.

---

#### **6. Work Completed**

As of June 18, 2025, the following tasks have been completed:

- **User Interface (UI) Design:** The app’s user interface has been finalized and approved by the design team. All main screens, including the homepage, product pages, and checkout flow, have been created.
    
- **Backend Development:** The backend architecture has been fully developed, including the database schema, API endpoints, and server configuration.
    
- **Core Features Implemented:**
    
    - User authentication
        
    - Product catalog management
        
    - Shopping cart functionality
        
- **Quality Assurance:** Initial QA testing has been completed for the core features. No major bugs were found during this phase.
    

---

#### **7. Current Issues**

- **Third-Party Payment Integration:** Integration with external payment gateways (PayPal, Stripe) has encountered issues, particularly with the handling of error responses. The team is working on a solution and expects this to be resolved within the next two weeks.
    
- **Device Compatibility:** There have been reports of the app's interface not rendering correctly on certain Android devices. This issue is currently under investigation by the front-end development team.
    

---

#### **8. Next Steps**

- **Beta Testing:** The beta version of the app will be made available to a select group of users by July 2, 2025. Feedback will be collected to identify any usability issues or bugs before the final release.
    
- **Bug Fixing and Optimizations:** The development team will continue to address integration issues with payment services and resolve the device compatibility problems. Additionally, performance optimizations will be performed to improve load times and app stability.
    
- **Launch Preparation:** Marketing materials, including the app’s promotional website and social media content, are being prepared in anticipation of the official launch on August 15, 2025.
    

---

#### **9. Conclusion**

The mobile app development project is progressing well, with major milestones completed on time. Although some technical challenges remain, the team is confident that these will be addressed in the coming weeks. The project is still on track to launch within the designated time frame.

---

#### **10. Recommendations**

- **Focus on Payment Integration:** The payment system integration issue should be prioritized to avoid delays in the beta testing phase.
    
- **Conduct Additional Device Testing:** More extensive testing across different Android devices is necessary to ensure consistent performance.
    

---

#### **11. References**

- Jira Project Management Logs
    
- User Testing Reports
    
- Design Team Specifications
    

---
